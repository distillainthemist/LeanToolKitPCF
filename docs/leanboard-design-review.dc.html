§6 Q5 — one control, two scopes

One component, one grammar, a scope segmented control in its toolbar: "This initiative" (what the Actions card shows today) or an org. Per-initiative hides the group rows and the org/pillar filters; org scope adds them and groups by initiative. Building two controls guarantees they drift.

Canvas card builder (R14)

The core problem: the canvas isn't a canvas. It's a read-only preview, and every layout decision is made in the right panel through abstract dropdowns ("1 column wide", "Height 2"). A person laying out a form looks at the shape they want, then has to translate it into numbers in a list somewhere else, and look back to check. The promise of "add fields and lay them out within the canvas" is only met if the canvas is where you drag, resize, and reorder.

Findings — canvas
  1. No direct manipulation. No drag to move, no resize handles, no snap — layout lives in the inspector's number dropdowns.
  2. No grid affordance. Column count is never shown, so "1 column wide" is a fraction of an unknown whole. No gridlines, no gutters, no empty cells.
  3. No drop target. The bottom half of the card is dead white space; "+ Add field" lives at the end of a long scroll in the other pane.
  4. No selection link. Nothing on the canvas shows which field the inspector is editing, and clicking a field on the canvas isn't the way in.
  5. It doesn't preview. KPIs is a mini table with two configured columns and renders as an empty box identical to a text field — the layout preview can't show layout.
  6. Empty states are inconsistent and wrong. Sponsor shows its prompt ("Click to select the sponsor"), everything else shows an em dash. In build mode a field should advertise its type, not its emptiness.
  7. Required is invisible on the canvas — the one property with real consequence.
  8. No typo guard: "Facilitior" ships straight through; labels have no validation, no duplicate-label warning.
Findings — inspector
  1. Everything is expanded at once. Each field is a six-control block; eight fields is a very long scroll with no overview and no way to see order at a glance.
  2. The internal key outranks the label — "business_impac" is truncated in its own box, styled as prominently as the human name. Developer detail, user-facing weight.
  3. Abstract sizing units. "Height 2" of what? No live effect, no preview while choosing.
  4. No field type visible until a field is selected — the list can't be scanned for structure.
  5. No undo, no unsaved-changes cue, and Save/Cancel float over the canvas at bottom-right with Cancel oddly detached.
